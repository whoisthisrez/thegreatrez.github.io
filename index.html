About

I'm Reza, a developer who likes software that runs and is used. Most of my work lives inside Telegram — bots that manage chats, fetch and convert media, answer with AI, or just host a good game.

I work mainly in Go when I want speed, strong concurrency and a single binary, and in Python when the tooling ecosystem makes it the fastest path. I care about clean structure, real tests, and code that stays fast under load.

When something isn't a fit, I'll say so. When it is, I'll ship it.
